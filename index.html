HELIX — crypto markets, paper trading and a research bot

HELIX is a mobile-first crypto app: live markets and coin pages, a paper-trading account with simulated money, a self-custody testnet wallet, and a Trading Bot that researches strategies and only trades on paper. Real-money trading is disabled.
