## Strategy Rules for Creator Outreach
1. **Never pay for initial reviews:** Paying for app reviews kills trust. Building relationships gets you organic screentime.
2. **Follow up once:** If they don't reply in 4 days, send a 1-sentence bump: *"Just checking to see if you managed to log in — let me know if you hit any bugs!"*
3. **If they make a video:** Put their logo or quote on the main landing page as Social Proof.
