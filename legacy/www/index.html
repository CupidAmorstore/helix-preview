FAQ

Common Questions

Does HELIX hold funds or private keys?

No. HELIX is a market intelligence dashboard. It does not hold funds, manage keys, or execute transactions.

Is there a real wallet or exchange sync?

Not yet. This preview focuses on market intelligence only. Read-only portfolio tracking is planned for a future phase.

Are the charts live?

Yes. Price data streams via Binance WebSocket, and 24-hour stats refresh via CoinGecko. Data sources are labeled in the UI.

How does access work for the desktop app?

Desktop builds are not publicly available yet. You can request access and we will follow up manually.

Does HELIX provide financial advice?

No. All analysis is informational only and does not guarantee outcomes. Use at your own risk.
